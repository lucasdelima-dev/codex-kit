# Code of Conduct

Codex Kit welcomes constructive technical collaboration.

Participants are expected to communicate professionally and treat other
contributors with respect.

## Expected behavior

- discuss technical disagreements based on evidence;
- provide actionable review feedback;
- respect different levels of experience;
- keep discussions relevant to the project;
- respect privacy and confidential information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
intimidation, disclosure of private information and knowingly introducing
malicious code or credentials are not acceptable.

## Technical disagreements

Prefer reproducible evidence, tests, benchmarks, source documentation and
small proofs of concept. Critique the implementation, not the person.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate or remove contributions that violate this Code of
Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in restrictions from
participation in the project.
